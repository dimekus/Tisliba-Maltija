Istruzzjonijiet

Kif tilgħab

Mexxi l-ittri lejn il-kaxxi bojod. Meta tkun lest agħfas Iċċekkja. L-ittri tajbin jibqgħu fil-grilja; dawk ħżiena jerġgħu lura biex terġa’ tużahom.

Punti

+1 għal kull ittra tajba u −1 għal kull ittra mhux f’postha. Jekk tpoġġi l-ħames ittri tajbin fl-istess turn tieħu bonus ta’ +5. Kelma kompluta tagħti bonus skont it-tul tagħha. Fir-rendikont tidher ukoll linja separata bil-punti li jkunu tnaqqsu minħabba l-għajnuna.

Ibdel

Ibiddel biss l-ittri li għadek ma użajtx. Jidher prompt li jispjega l-għajnuna. Jekk tagħżel Uża l-għajnuna, tara riklam qasir u l-ittri jinbidlu awtomatikament. Agħlaq jerġgħek għat-tisliba mingħajr riklam jew għajnuna.

🇬🇧 English

Meta tixgħelha, fit-tkabbir tal-ħjiel tidher it-traduzzjoni bl-Ingliż. Ir-risposta xorta trid tiddaħħal bil-Malti. Kull darba li terġa’ tixgħelha teħtieġ riklam.

GĦ · IE · H

Agħżel waħda biex tikxef il-kaxxi fejn tintuża. Kull għażla teħtieġ riklam separat. Jekk ma tkunx fit-tisliba, tingħata avviż u ma jintweriex riklam.

Joker

Għandek 3 jokers f’kull turn. Kull wieħed jikxef ittra bla punti. Wara t-tlieta tista’ tara riklam biex tingħata joker bonus wieħed għal dak it-turn.

Ikxifli Kelma

Wara r-riklam, agħżel kaxxa fil-kelma li trid tikxef. Jekk il-kaxxa qiegħda f’intersezzjoni, agħżel jekk tridx il-kelma orizzontali jew vertikali. Il-kelma kollha tinkixef. Jitnaqqas punt biss għal kull kaxxa li ma tkunx diġà mimlija bl-ittra t-tajba; l-ittri li jkunu f’posthom ma jnaqqsux punti. L-ittri mikxufa jitneħħew mill-għażla tat-turns li jmiss.

Għaqqadli t-tisliba

Din tlesti l-ittri li jkun fadal u ttemm il-livell bl-għajnuna. Wara l-għażla jintwera riklam. Jekk tkun poġġejt 0–5 ittri tajbin jitnaqqsulek 15-il punt; jekk tkun poġġejt aktar minn 5, jitnaqqas punt għal kull kelma li jkun fadal, sa massimu ta’ 10 punti.

Fissirli

Fi tmiem il-livell agħżel Fissirli u mbagħad agħfas kelma fit-tisliba. Tista’ tara t-tifsira, sentenza u traduzzjoni. Meta tlesti agħfas Waqqaf Fissirli.

Oħroġ mil-logħba

Tista’ toħroġ fi kwalunkwe ħin. Il-progress, il-punti u l-ittri fil-grilja jiġu ssejvjati. Meta terġa’ tiftaħ il-logħba, agħżel jekk tridx tkompli minn fejn ħallejt jew tibda logħba ġdida.

How to play

Move the letter tiles into the white squares. When ready, press the Maltese button shown below. Correct letters stay in the grid; letters in the wrong place return so you can use them again.

Scoring

You earn +1 for each correct letter and lose 1 point for each letter placed in the wrong square. Placing all five letters correctly in one turn gives a +5 bonus. Completing a word gives an additional bonus based on its length. The turn summary also shows a separate line for points deducted because help was used.

Change

This changes only the letters you have not used. A prompt explains the help. Choose Use help to watch a short advertisement; the letters then change automatically. Choose Close to return without an advertisement or help.

🇬🇧 English

When enabled, enlarging a clue also shows the English translation. You must still enter the answer in Maltese. A new advertisement is required each time you switch this option on again.

GĦ · IE · silent H

Choose one to reveal every square where it is used. Each choice requires a separate advertisement, even when that combination is not used in the current crossword. If you have removed advertisements, the check happens immediately.

Joker

You receive 3 jokers in every turn. Each joker reveals one letter without awarding a point. After using all three, you may watch an advertisement to receive one extra joker for that turn.

Reveal a word — Ikxifli Kelma

After the advertisement, select a square in the word you want to reveal. At a crossing, choose the horizontal or vertical word. The whole word is revealed. One point is deducted only for each square that does not already contain the correct letter; correctly placed letters carry no deduction. Its revealed letters are removed from future turns.

Complete my crossword

This fills all remaining letters and ends the level with help. An advertisement is shown after you confirm. If you placed 0–5 correct letters, 15 points are deducted. If you placed more than 5, one point is deducted for each unfinished word, up to a maximum of 10 points.

Explain it

At the end of a level press the Maltese Fissirli button shown below, then select a word in the crossword. You can view its meaning, an example sentence and its translation. When finished, press Waqqaf Fissirli.

Exit the game

You can leave at any time. Your progress, score and letters in the grid are saved. When you reopen the game, choose whether to continue where you left off or start a new game.
